Anaemia Among Women in Nigeria: Public Health Analysis

Power BIExcelData CleaningData VisualizationHealthcare AnalyticsNDHS 2018

Problem. Anaemia affects women's health and maternal outcomes, but cases are not spread evenly across communities. Which groups carry the most cases, and where are the gaps in iron supplementation?

What I did

  • Prepared a 33,924-row survey extract in Excel (17 variables: wealth, education, residence, births, haemoglobin, iron intake) and trimmed it to 12 analysis columns.
  • Removed non-anaemic and blank records, leaving 4,415 respondents with mild, moderate or severe anaemia.
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard and presented findings in a 7-slide deck.

What I found

  • Moderate anaemia was the most common level (2,358 of 4,415 cases, 53%).
  • The poorest wealth group had the most cases; the richest had the fewest.
  • Rural respondents had more cases than urban respondents.
  • 57% had their first birth between ages 12 and 19. Average haemoglobin: 104.08 g/L.
  • 81% were not taking iron pills, sprinkles or syrup, and iron use rose with wealth: 10.8% in the poorest group against 28.5% in the richest.

Recommendation. Focus screening, iron-supplement awareness, and nutrition education on poorer and rural communities, and build anaemia education into existing maternal and child-health programmes, with a focus on women in their 20s and early 30s.

Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

Goal. Show management where revenue comes from across branches, cities, product lines, gender and customer type.

What I did

  • Built an interactive Excel dashboard on 1,000 transactions (unit price, quantity, tax, sales, revenue).
  • Summarised performance with pivot tables and charts by city, product line and customer segment.

What I found

  • Total revenue of about $351K across 5,510 units sold.
